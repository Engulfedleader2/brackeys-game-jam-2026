## The Game

**Brackeys GameJam 2026** is a small card game inspired by the bluffing of **BS and the risk of busting in **Blackjack**.

Players take turns adding cards to a shared pile while trying to keep the total from going **over 10**.

---

## How to Play

1. Players take turns playing one card valued **1–5**.
2. Each card adds to the shared pile's running total.
3. Push the pile **over 10** and you Bust.
4. Playing a **1** gives you the option to place it **face-down**.
5. Don't have a 1? You can play **any card face-down** and claim that it's a 1.
6. Other players won't know whether you told the truth.
7. The hidden card is revealed.

The main gamejam mode.

Play against AI opponents capable of:

* Bluffing about their cards
